# Code of Conduct

Project participants must provide a respectful, harassment-free environment
regardless of identity, experience, background or viewpoint. Technical
disagreement is expected; personal attacks, intimidation, discrimination,
doxxing and sustained disruption are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
Report conduct concerns privately to `conduct@nigenje.com`. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical and without retaliation.

This concise policy applies to project repositories, issue trackers, review
systems, chat spaces and project events.
